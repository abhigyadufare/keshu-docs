# Collection and search pages

## Collection banner

Each collection page starts with a banner holding the collection's name and description.

- **Banner color scheme**: the colors of the banner panel.
- **Show illustration**: a simple drawing on the right of the banner, in the scheme's colors.
- **Banner background image**: a photo behind the banner, with the text on a card over it.
  Replaces the illustration. To use a different photo per collection, connect this setting
  to a collection metafield (see [Custom data](README.md#custom-data-keshu-can-use)).
- **Show collection image**: shows the collection's own image beside the heading. Hidden
  when the banner has a background image.
- **Show collection description**

## Products

- **Products per page** and **Columns on desktop** (phones show two).
- **Pagination**: **Page numbers**, or a **Load more button** that adds the next products
  below without leaving the page.

Customers can switch between a grid and a list with the buttons next to sorting.

## Filters and sorting

Filters come from your product data: availability, price, vendor, product type, tags,
variant options (like Size and Color), and metafields. Choose which filters show, and in what
order, in the free **Search and Discovery** app by Shopify. There you can also show color
filters as swatches.

- **Show filters** and **Show sorting**
- **Filter layout on desktop**: a **Sidebar** beside the products, or a **Drawer** that opens
  from a Filter button. Phones always use the drawer.
- **Open filter groups**: **All**, **First two**, or **None** start open. A group with a
  selected filter is always open.
- **Values shown before "Show more"**: for filters with many values, like tags or vendors.
- **Show product counts**: the number of products next to each value.
- **Show names next to swatches**: turn off to show color and image filters as a row of
  swatches only.
- **Show alphabetical sorting** and **Show sorting by date**: extra sorting choices.

The search page has the same filter and sorting settings, plus **Results per page**. Search
results include products, articles, and pages, each labeled.

## Search as you type

The search field in the header suggests products, collections, articles, and pages as the
customer types, plus search terms they might mean. It uses Shopify's search, so it finds what
your store's search finds.
