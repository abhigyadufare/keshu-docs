# Sections

Add a section in the theme editor with **Add section**. Every section below has a **Color
scheme** (see [Colors](theme-settings.md#colors)) and **Top padding** and **Bottom padding**
settings (None, Small, Medium, Large), so they aren't repeated in each entry.

Many sections start with a **Heading** and a **Text** block:

- **Heading**: in the heading, words you make *italic* show in the scheme's accent color.
  **Size** is Extra large, Large, Medium, or Small.
- **Text**: a paragraph, in Large, Medium, or Small.
- **Buttons**: up to two buttons, each with a label and a link. The first is filled, the second
  outlined. A button with no label is hidden.

Leave a heading or text block empty to hide it.

## Header and footer

### Announcement bar

A strip above the header with up to 6 announcements. Visitors step through them with the
arrows. Each **Announcement** has **Text** and an optional **Link**.

### Header

- **Logo image** and **Logo width**. Without a logo, the header shows your store name.
- **Logo position on large screens**: Left, or Center (the menu sits on its own row below).
- **Menu**: the header menu. See [Set up menus](README.md#3-set-up-menus).
- **Customer account menu**: the menu shown to signed-in customers.
- **Keep header visible while scrolling**

The header also has search (with suggestions as you type), a link to customer accounts when
they're turned on, and the cart.

### Footer

Add up to 5 blocks: **Menu** (a heading and a menu), **Text** (a heading and text), and
**Email signup** (a heading, text, and a signup field that adds the customer to your email
list). Below them:

- **Show social media icons**: links from Theme settings → Social media.
- **Show Follow on Shop**: lets customers follow your store in the Shop app. Needs Shop Pay.
- **Show country/region selector**: add countries in Settings → Markets first.
- **Show language selector**: add languages in Settings → Languages first.
- **Show payment icons**: the payment methods your store accepts.
- **Show policy links**: links to the policies in Settings → Policies.

## Home page and landing pages

### Slideshow

Up to 6 slides. Each **Slide** has:

- **Background**: an **Image** (with an optional **Mobile image** for phones) or a
  **Video**. A video can be uploaded or a YouTube or Vimeo link; it plays without sound, on a
  loop, and visitors can pause it.
- **Text position**: Left, Center, Bottom left, or Bottom center.
- **Text color scheme**: colors for the panel behind the text.
- Heading, Text, and Buttons blocks.

Section settings:

- **Layout**: **Text on image**, or **Text beside image** (image on one side, text on the other).
- **Height**: Small, Medium, or Large. Large fills the first screen on computers.
- **Use full page width**
- **Change slides automatically** and **Change slides every**. Autoplay pauses when a visitor
  hovers, focuses a slide, or presses pause. It never runs for visitors who turn on reduced
  motion.
- **Slideshow name**: read out by screen readers. Defaults to your store name.

### Featured collection

Products from a collection in a grid. Add a second **Collection** block and it becomes
**tabs**, one per collection (up to 5). Each block has a **Collection** and a **Tab label**
(the collection name if empty).

Settings: **Heading**, **Text**, **Products to show**, and **Columns on desktop** (phones show
two). A **View all** button shows when the collection has more products than shown.

### Featured product

A product with its gallery and details, like a small product page. It has the same blocks as
the [product page](product-page.md), plus **Thumbnail position** and **Image zoom**.

### Collection circles

Round images that link to collections, in a row that scrolls sideways. Up to 12. Each
**Collection** block has a **Collection**, an optional **Image** (the collection image if
empty), and an optional **Label** (the collection name if empty).

### Feature cards

Cards with an image, heading, text, and link. Up to 8. Set **Columns on desktop**. Each
**Card** has an **Image**, **Heading**, **Text**, **Link**, **Link label**, and its own
**Color scheme**.

### Bento grid

Tiles of different sizes that fit together in a grid. Up to 10. Each **Tile** has an
**Image** (tiles without one show their color scheme), **Heading**, **Text**, **Link**,
**Size** (Small, Wide, Tall, or Large), and **Color scheme**. The whole tile is a link.

### Icons with text

A row of short points, like "Free shipping over $75" or "Made in Portland". Up to 6. Each
block has an **Icon** (Truck, Return arrow, Gift, Leaf, Heart, Star, Lock, Clock, Chat bubble,
Check mark, or None) and **Text**.

### Image with text

An image beside rich content. Set **Image** and **Image position** (Left or Right). Add
Heading, Text, Buttons, and app blocks on the text side.

### Rich text

Text on its own, for a short story or a statement. **Alignment**: Left or Center. Add Heading,
Text, Buttons, and app blocks.

### Video

A video that loads only when someone presses play, so it doesn't slow down the page.

- **Video** (uploaded) or **Video link** (YouTube or Vimeo). An uploaded video wins.
- **Cover image**: shown until the video plays. Uploaded videos use their own cover if empty.
- **Video description**: read out by screen readers.
- **Use full page width**

### Shoppable gallery

Photos or short videos of your products in use, like customer photos, in a row that scrolls
sideways. Up to 12.

- **Photo**: an **Image** (use photos you have permission to share), a **Caption**, and a
  **Product shown**, which adds a product link under the photo.
- **Video**: an uploaded **Video** or a **Video link**. It plays without sound, on a loop,
  while it's on screen, with a pause button. Also has **Caption** and **Product shown**.

### Shop the look

A photo with dots on the products in it. Pressing a dot shows the product, its price, a short
description, and an **Add** or **Choose options** button. On phones, it opens from the bottom
of the screen.

- **Image**: keeps its shape on every screen, so the dots stay in place.
- **Content position**: Above the photo, Left of the photo, or Right of the photo. Phones
  always show it above.
- **Hotspot style**: **Plus**, **Number** (matches the numbered product list), or **Price**.
- Each **Hotspot** has a **Product** and a **Horizontal position** and **Vertical position**
  in percent. Drag the sliders while watching the preview to place it. Up to 8.
- The content area holds Heading, Text, Buttons, Custom Liquid, and **Products in the look**,
  a numbered list of the products with their prices and buttons.

### Before and after

Two photos with a handle to slide between them. Good for results, like skin before and after
a routine, or a room before and after. Use two images of the same size.
Settings: **Before image**, **Before label**, **After image**, **After label**. Visitors can
also move the handle with the arrow keys.

### Testimonials

Customer quotes in a row that scrolls sideways. Up to 12. Each **Quote** has the **Quote**, a
**Name**, a **Detail** (like what they bought or where they live), and a **Star rating** (0
hides the stars). Use real quotes from real customers.

### FAQ

Questions that open to show their answers. Each **Question** block has a **Question** and an
**Answer**.

### Logo list

Logos of places that stock you or wrote about you. Up to 12. Each **Logo** has an **Image**, a
**Name** (read out by screen readers when the image has no alt text), and a **Link**.

### Countdown timer

A timer counting down to a date, for a real sale or launch. Add Heading, Text, and Buttons
blocks.

- **End date** (YYYY-MM-DD) and **End time** (24-hour, like 18:00), in your store's time zone.
- **Message after the end**: replaces the timer once the date has passed.

The timer doesn't show until you set an end date.

### Email signup

A signup field that adds customers to your email list (Customers in your admin). Add Heading,
Text, and app blocks.

### Custom Liquid

For app snippets or your own code. Code that doesn't work can break the page, so
**duplicate your theme first** (Online Store → Themes → ⋯ → Duplicate) and try it there.

## Other pages

- **Product recommendations** (product page): **Heading** and **Products to show**. Shopify
  picks related products from your sales and product data.
- **Contact form** (contact page): a **Heading** above the form. Messages go to your store's
  email address.
- **Password** (password page): **Logo** and **Logo width**. The message comes from Online
  Store → Preferences.
- **Blog**: **Articles per page** and **Show tag filter** (buttons to filter by tag).
- **Article**: **Show author**. Comments show when they're turned on for the blog.
- **Collections** (the list of all collections): **Collections per page**.
